Privacy Policy for Cookie Cleaner

Last updated: October 07, 2025

Overview

Cookie Cleaner is a browser extension that helps users manage and delete cookies. This privacy policy explains how the extension works and what data it accesses.

Data Collection

Cookie Cleaner does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.

What the Extension Does

  • Reads cookies from websites you visit to display cookie counts
  • Deletes cookies only when you explicitly click the delete buttons
  • All operations are performed locally in your browser

Data Storage

  • No data is stored by this extension
  • No data is sent to external servers
  • No analytics or tracking is performed
  • All cookie operations happen entirely within your browser

Permissions Explained

  • cookies: Required to read and delete cookies from websites
  • activeTab: Required to identify the current website's domain
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): Required to access cookies from any website you choose to clear

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be posted here and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
